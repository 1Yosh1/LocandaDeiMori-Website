# Marketing Strategy: Capturing Taormina's Luxury Market

## 1. Targeted Audience
- **Primary**: International luxury travelers (US, UK, Northern Europe).
- **Secondary**: Local foodies and destination wedding planners.

## 2. SEO & Local Visibility
- **Keyword Targets**: "Best view restaurant Taormina", "Traditional Sicilian fine dining", "Locanda Dei Mori bookings".
- **GMB (Google My Business)**: Keep photos updated with high-end, professionally edited food photography.
- **Rich Snippets**: Implementation of Review and Recipe schema to dominate search results.

## 3. Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Sophisticated, welcoming, and deeply rooted in Sicilian tradition.
- **Visuals**: High contrast, warm lighting, focus on "Mori" (Moorish) aesthetics.

## 4. Digital Conversions
- **Landing Page**: Must convert in < 3 clicks.
- **Urgency**: Subtle use of "Limited Seating" for terrace tables.
